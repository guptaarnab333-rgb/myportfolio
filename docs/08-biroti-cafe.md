## Context

> *"Cafés became offices. Tables became desks. The person across from you became background noise."*

> *Screens didn't replace conversation — they just made it optional. And optional became rare.*

---

## Insight

> **Being present is no longer the default. It has to be designed.**

Eye contact. A sentence that goes somewhere. Silence that isn't filled. These don't happen by accident anymore — they happen by intention.

---

## Naming

**Biroti** — /bɪˈrɔti/ — Bengali for **pause**.

> Not a stop. Not a break. A pause — the beat between two things that gives both of them meaning.

## Website Sections

### 01 — Philosophy
> *"We created this space because we were tired of being somewhere else while sitting together."*

> *"The phone is the most powerful object of the 21st century — a library, a camera, an infinite social gathering — and it has quietly annexed every idle moment we have. We don't hate technology. We use it to take your reservation."*

> *"What we believe in is simple: the most nourishing thing you can experience inside a café is the unbroken attention of another person. Eye contact. A sentence that goes somewhere. Silence that isn't awkward."*

### 02 — The House Rules

| # | Rule | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Leave the phone in your pocket.** | Calls, cameras, scrolling — all kept away from the table. If something urgent arises, our lobby is always available. We're not the last café in the world; we're just a different one. |
| **02** | **No laptops, no work.** | Mornings and afternoons belong to conversation and ritual. We want you to focus on human connections rather than always working mindlessly. |
| **03** | **Linger — we mean it.** | There is no table turnover. Order what you like, stay as long as feels right. The only thing we ask in return is that you are somewhere in your body, here in this room, for the duration of your stay. |

### 04 — The Space

**Designed for the person who wants to be here.**

#### 01 — The Main Room
Sixteen seats. Natural light from the north-facing windows. Tables spaced wide enough for a full conversation. The floors are original — **1940s terrazzo** that someone took great care not to ruin. We did the same.

#### 02 — The Reading Nook
A corner with a library. **Borrow any book, leave any book.** If you find one you need to keep, we ask only that you replace it with something you've already finished.

#### 03 — Monthly Events
We host a monthly **adda** — an informal gathering around a theme, a person, or a question. *No recordings. What is said in the room stays in the room.* Sign up at the counter.

#### 04 — Private Hire
Available on **Sunday mornings.** For small gatherings where the quality of conversation matters more than the headcount. We close the room, set the table, and stay out of the way.

## Portfolio Framing

A confident, opinionated brand-identity case study. The **concept is the hero** — lead with the insight quote.

**Order for the case study page:**
1. Hero — the wordmark on parchment background, or one of the conversation prompt cards
2. Context + Insight (set as a large pull-quote moment)
3. Naming explanation
4. Tagline
5. Logo system — three background variants
6. Color palette — large swatches with hex values
7. Typography specimen
8. Touchpoints carousel:
   - Menu mockup
   - Conversation prompt cards (one per prompt, scrollable)
   - Stamps & real-world product
9. Website walk-through — Philosophy → Rules → Menu → Space → Reservation
10. Brand principles list
11. Closing: *"No photographs, please."*

The voice is the most distinctive thing in this project — preserve every quote verbatim on the website. This is a tonal benchmark for the rest of the portfolio.
