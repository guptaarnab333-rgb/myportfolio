## Theme

> **Explored Form Derivation Influenced by Sound, Emotions, Nature, & Morphing Forms.**

The featured works showcase how these inspirations are brought to life as dynamic forms, incorporating a range of materials and textures.

Projects explore forms through materials like **high-density thermocol, sunboards, and others**, emphasizing creative treatments and distinct textural variations.

The focus lies on the **morphing and transformation of forms**, embodying the essence of natural elements, emotional expressions, and sound waves.

---

## Process

### 1. Sound Interpretation Activity
Listened and observed a given sound track in order to create forms out of it.

### 2. Group Activity
Scribbling and colouring forms collaboratively to discover candidate shapes.

### 3. Form Selection
- **Major form** — selected from the chosen form inspired by the sound-driven exploration
- **Minor form** — chose another form by getting inspired by nature

### 4. Morphing Study
Showed the morphing of both forms (major + minor) **on a single sheet** — a visual interpolation between the two.

---

## Material Explorations

### Sunboard Build
- **Making of the forms** — shows the morphing process of both forms from a sunboard
- **Treatment** — treatment of the form with **sunboard dust and colour** to provide texture
- **Final morphing project** — completed with sunboard

### Thermocol Build
- **Morphed forms** — shows the morphing of both forms with thermocol
- **Treatment** — treatment of the form with **white cement** to give it a wave-like texture, and use of **blue colors** to make it more wave-like
- **Final morphing project** — completed with thermocol
