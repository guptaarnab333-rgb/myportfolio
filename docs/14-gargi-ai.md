## Discover Phase

### Methodology
An online survey was created and conducted via **Google Forms** with a sample size of **n = 100 respondents**, selected randomly.

### Results

| Stat | Insight |
|---|---|
| **85%** | of respondents stated they would prefer an **AI mentor for sensitive career or personal questions** over a human contact due to guaranteed anonymity |
| **90%** | of users rated *"immediate access to locally relevant emergency and government helplines"* as a **top-three necessity** |
| **35%** (only) | could reliably recall more than **two Uttarakhand-specific contact numbers** |
| **78%** | of users reported feeling their formal complaints were **ignored or lost** when submitted via traditional email or paper channels |
| **90%** | stated that **real-time status tracking was essential** to build institutional trust |

### Synthesis
The data points to four needs:
1. **Anonymous AI counsel** (85% trust an AI more than a human for sensitive questions)
2. **Locally-relevant emergency access** (Uttarakhand-specific helplines, not generic national ones)
3. **Transparent grievance filing** (real-time tracking, not lost in email)
4. **Empowerment-first framing** (not a complaint app — a mentor)

## User Experience — Interface Ergonomics

Mobile-first interactions designed for **thumb-zone reachability**, large tap targets, and minimal cognitive load — appropriate for a user demographic that may be approaching the app under stress (e.g. filing a grievance) or in an emergency (e.g. SOS).

---

## Prototyped Screens

### 1. Onboarding — *Seamless Onboarding*
> Streamlining the login process with options to Login either via **Login ID & password** or through **Facebook or Google**.

### 2. Grievance — *File and Track Grievance*
> Fearlessly file and track complaints through a transparent and secure logged process. It guarantees user empowerment by providing **real-time status updates** on their reported issues.

### 3. Helplines — *Access Support*
> Get **immediate access to critical support**, featuring both essential national helplines and specific **emergency and government contacts relevant to Uttarakhand**.

### 4. Chat Bot — *Chat with Gargi*
> The Gargi AI is your **confidential mentor**, providing immediate, non-judgmental support for all matters concerning empowerment and self-advocacy.

### 5. Profile / Mission Hub
> Personal hub where you can manage account details, language settings, and set personalized app preferences. It is also the central, secure location to **review and read about the Project Gargi mission** for complete transparency.
