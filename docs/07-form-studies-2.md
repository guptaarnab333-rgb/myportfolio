## Sections / Sequence

### 1. Different Types of Folding Techniques
Exploration of various paper-folding methods — likely covering valley, mountain, accordion, and modular folds. Each technique yields different structural and visual qualities.

### 2. Form Derived from Nature Using Paper Folding
Translation of a natural form into a folded-paper structure — observation of nature → abstraction → fold sequence → physical model.

### 3. Two Similar Forms Made with Different Materials Derived from Personality
The same form executed in **two different material languages**, with the material choice driven by a personality-based study. Demonstrates how material changes the read of an identical geometry.

### 4. Lighting Fixture — Final Product
The culminating deliverable: a **lamp / lighting fixture** built from the folding studies. The folded form, illuminated, becomes a functional object — not just a sculptural exercise.

---

## Continuation from Form Studies 1

Where **Studies 1** used sunboard and thermocol, exploring morphing forms inspired by sound, emotion, and nature → **Studies 2** progresses into the craft of paper folding with a **functional outcome** (lighting fixture) rather than a purely sculptural one.

The arc across both:
- Studies 1: *Idea → form → material → texture → exhibition object*
- Studies 2: *Technique → natural form → personality-driven material → functional product*
