## Introduction

My desk in the hostel often becomes cluttered from studying, eating, and storage, making it hard to stay organized and productive. This project aims to design a solution for hostel students facing similar challenges.

---

## Problem Statement

> Hostelers often face trouble with workspace cleanliness and organization due to limited work and storage space being used for multiple tasks, resulting in decreased productivity and clutter.

---

## Methodology

**Double Diamond Design Approach** — Discover → Define → Develop → Deliver

| Panel size | Timeline | Process |
|---|---|---|
| Individual project | 4 weeks | Double Diamond Design Approach |

---

## Discover Phase

### Desk Research
Used the **5W method** — What, Who, Where, When, Why — to analyze the problem. Built user journey maps, mind maps, and benchmarks of existing single-function products and identified space-related market issues.

### Field Research
Conducted surveys and interviews with hostel students to understand their workspace challenges — direct insights into desk usage, clutter issues, and the need for a more organized, functional workspace.

### Key Insights

| Stat | Insight |
|---|---|
| **91%** | think lack of storage impacts productivity |
| **100%** | current state of workspace affects focus and ability to complete tasks |
| **73%** | find workspace cluttered or disorganized |
| **64%** | find it challenging to keep workspace clean and organized |

**Qualitative insights:**
- Most hostel students said they eat food, watch movies, do paintings or sketching alongside studying.
- Most said they find it very **frustrating, annoying, and lazy** while switching between tasks.

---

## Define Phase

### 5 Whys Analysis
1. Why do hostel students need a product that attaches to their table?
2. Why is their workspace becoming cluttered?
3. Why is there a lack of storage and how does it affect them?
4. Why is it important to have a clutter-free workspace?
5. Why does this impact productivity?

### Design Brief
> To design a simple product that allows efficient and secure freeing-up of space and organization, eliminating the need to constantly move things from the desk while switching between tasks.

---

## Develop Phase

### Brainstorming
Generated ideas for solutions that **don't require complex components like hinges or screws** — focusing on simplicity and ease of use.

### Ideation Sketches
Multiple ideation sketches were generated; several were rejected before arriving at the final concept.

### Final Concept
> This design can be made out of a **single metal sheet, and then bent to give its shape, without any joints.**

This is the product's most distinctive moment — jointless, screwless, single-material construction.

### Prototyping
- **Low-fidelity Prototype 1** — first physical exploration
- **Low-fidelity Prototype 2** — second iteration
- **Low-fidelity Prototype 3** — third iteration
- **Mid-fidelity Prototype** — closer to final form

---

## Deliver / Testing Phase

### Real-Life Usage & Testing
Before/After testing of the prototype on a real hostel desk.

### Feedback Received
- Too many sharp corners and edges
- Less use of material
- The overall flow of the form should have been better

### Proposed Changes (next iteration)
- Less sharp corners and edges
- Removal of lower part
- Make holes on the middle portion and the sides
- Make it with a thinner sheet

---

## Conclusion

> This project taught me the value of user-centered design — helping me create practical solutions that improve workspace organization and productivity for hostel students.
