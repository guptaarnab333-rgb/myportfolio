## Overview

The School of Design, Doon University organized **"Ganga Gatha"** — a road painting competition under the **Namami Gange initiative** — with the objective of raising awareness about the endangered species associated with the River Ganga.

The competition focused on highlighting the **rich biodiversity of the river ecosystem**, particularly species that are under threat due to environmental degradation and human activities.

---

## Participation

- **Total teams:** 9
- Each team was assigned a **specific endangered species** of the Ganga as their subject

---

| # | Species |
|---|---|
| 1 | River Skimmer |
| 2 | Ganges River Dolphin |
| 3 | Northern River Terrapin |
| 4 | Golden Mahseer |
| 5 | River Tern |
| 6 | Sarus Crane |
| 7 | Smooth-coated Otter |
| 8 | **Ganges River Shark** ⭐ (winning team) |
| 9 | Fishing Cat |

Each team visually interpreted their assigned species, depicting:
- Its habitat
- Its ecological significance
- The challenges it faces

---

## The Canvas

The **campus road was transformed into a large-scale open canvas**, where each artwork contributed to a collective narrative about conservation and coexistence.

The activity:
- Encouraged artistic expression
- Promoted awareness about the importance of protecting aquatic and riparian biodiversity
- Provided all necessary materials, allowing participants to focus entirely on **creativity, teamwork, and execution**
- Fostered collaboration and engagement among students
- Emphasised the role of design and art in **environmental communication**

---

## Documentation

The event was **documented through drone videography**, capturing the progression of artworks and the transformation of the space.

A **compiled video** was shared on the **official Namami Gange Instagram page** — extending the reach of the initiative and showcasing the participants' efforts to a wider audience.

---

## Result

🏆 **Winning Team:** "Ganges River Shark"

Awarded the first prize for their outstanding **concept, execution, and visual impact**.

---

## Impact / Statement

> Through this initiative, *Ganga Gatha* successfully utilized **public art as a medium to highlight critical environmental issues** — reinforcing the message of conservation and the need to protect the diverse species that depend on the Ganga.
