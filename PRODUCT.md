# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, design leads, and recruiters evaluating Arnab Gupta for a product design role or internship. They arrive skeptical and time-pressed, often scanning many portfolios in one sitting, frequently on mobile. The job they're trying to get done: decide in under a minute whether this person can think, can craft, and is worth a conversation. Secondary visitors are peers and collaborators who want to understand how Arnab approaches problems.

## Product Purpose

A single-page editorial portfolio that presents Arnab Gupta's product design work (case studies) with enough depth to prove rigor and enough restraint to respect the visitor's time. It exists to convert a cold viewer into an interview. Success looks like: a visitor reads at least one case study end-to-end, comes away with a clear sense of how Arnab thinks, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Warm and human, expressed through a dark editorial surface. Three words: **thoughtful, grounded, candid.** The voice is first-person and plainspoken, the voice of a designer explaining real decisions to a respected peer, not a brand performing for an audience. Warmth is carried by storytelling, honest framing of tradeoffs, and unhurried pacing, not by warm-tinted color. The work should feel made by a specific person with a point of view, never by an agency or a template.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS template**: no purple-to-blue gradients, no identical icon-heading-text card grids, no hero-metric stat blocks, no Inter-for-everything. The existing palette (deep ink, indigo accent, flame red) and type pairing already pushes away from this; keep it that way.
- **Cluttered / maximalist**: no competing focal points, no wall of sections, no visual noise. One clear thing to look at per viewport. Restraint is the point.
- **Corporate / sterile**: no stock photography, no faceless big-agency blandness. Personal voice and real work over polish-without-substance.
- **Trendy gimmicks**: no brutalism-for-its-own-sake, no scroll-jacking, no effects that fight readability or usability. Motion serves comprehension and delight, never spectacle.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the thinking, not just the screens.** Case studies prove reasoning and tradeoffs, not just pretty final shots. Depth is the differentiator.
2. **Respect the visitor's minute.** Every section earns its place; lead with the strongest work; never make a busy reader hunt for the point.
3. **One voice, first person.** Copy sounds like Arnab talking to a peer: candid about constraints, specific about decisions, free of marketing language.
4. **Craft is the argument.** For a design portfolio, execution quality *is* the portfolio. Typography, spacing, motion, and contrast must themselves demonstrate the skill being claimed.
5. **Warmth through substance, not decoration.** Human feeling comes from honest storytelling and considered pacing, not from warm colors or decorative flourish on a dark canvas.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Body text ≥4.5:1 against its background (watch muted gray on dark ink especially); large text ≥3:1. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus states. Every animation (cursor parallax, scroll reveals, React Spring transitions) needs a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative, typically an instant or crossfade fallback. Don't rely on color alone to convey meaning (accent indigo / flame red must pair with text or shape cues).
